Before you start, ask me for any values in square brackets that I haven't filled in. Ask for all of them in one message.

I need to decide [THE DECISION]. The options I'm considering are [MY OPTIONS].

1. Ask me what matters most to me in this decision, and wait for my answer.
2. Then compare the options against those criteria in a short table.
3. Point out any option I may have missed, and any assumption I'm making that's worth checking.
4. Tell me which option looks strongest and why, but leave the decision to me.
