Before you start, ask me for any values in square brackets that I haven't filled in. Ask for all of them in one message.

Quiz me on [TOPIC]. I'd describe my level as [MY LEVEL].

- Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer.
- After each answer, tell me whether I'm right and explain briefly.
- Make the next question harder if I got it right and easier if I didn't.
- After ten questions, tell me what I know well and what to study next.
