Before you start, ask me for any values in square brackets that I haven't filled in. Ask for all of them in one message.

I'm reading this because [WHY I'M READING IT]:

[PASTE TEXT]

Give me:
1. The main point in one sentence.
2. The three to five key points, each with the evidence the author gives for it.
3. Anything that's claimed without support, or that seems questionable.
4. What this means for why I'm reading it.

Use plain language and keep the whole summary under 300 words.
