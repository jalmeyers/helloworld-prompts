Before you start, ask me for any values in square brackets that I haven't filled in. Ask for all of them in one message.

Here are my rough notes from a meeting with [WHO I MET WITH]:

[PASTE NOTES]

Write a follow-up email I can send today. It should:

- Thank them in one line, without gushing.
- Recap what we agreed in 2–4 bullets.
- List next steps, each with an owner and a date.
- End with one clear ask.

Keep it under 150 words. Use a subject line that names the meeting topic.
