Before you start, ask me for any values in square brackets that I haven't filled in. Ask for all of them in one message.

I'm running a [LENGTH] meeting with [ATTENDEES]. The purpose is [MEETING PURPOSE].

Create an agenda that:
- Starts with the outcome we need by the end of the meeting.
- Lists each item with a time box, an owner and the decision or output it should produce.
- Leaves five minutes at the end for next steps.

Also suggest anything people should read or prepare beforehand.
